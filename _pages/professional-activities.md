## Current Academic Roles

- Associate Professor, Department of Applied Mathematics, Institute of Mathematics and Statistics, UERJ.
- Permanent faculty member and Deputy Coordinator, Graduate Program in Computational Sciences and Mathematical Modelling (COMPMAT), since October 2023 in the deputy role.
- Permanent faculty member, specialization program in Mathematical Learning, IME/UERJ.
- Chair of the COMPMAT admissions and scholarship committees since 2023; member of its council since 2020.
- Editor-in-Chief of *Cadernos do IME — Série Matemática* since 2024.
- Teacher in OME–OBMEP Mirim na Escola, IMPA, since 2025.
- Work with the Meninas Olímpicas do IMPA initiative since 2019.
- Advisor in Math en Jeans with SESI/RJ schools.
- Science-outreach fellow at Fundação Cecierj/FECTI since 2026.
